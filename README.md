**Midnight Vault** is a top-down stealth heist game. You are Wren, a thief
slipping into a guarded hall after dark. Cross the room, crack the vault,
grab the gems if you dare, and reach the getaway door before the alarm
hits 100%.

- **Read the room before you move:** three patrolling guards (Rook, Bishop,
  Knight) sweep yellow vision cones that walls cut off, and two security
  cameras sweep red cones across the hall and the east corridor.
- **Two routes:** the open hall is fast and exposed; the vent crawl along
  the top is slow but hides you from every cone below.
- **Tools at a cost:** smoke pellets [Q] hide you for a few seconds,
  an EMP [E] freezes the cameras — each one costs score.
- **The job:** stand at the vault for 3 seconds to pick the lock, then run
  for the GETAWAY door. Get spotted and the alarm climbs through CALM,
  SUSPICIOUS and HUNT; at 100% the hall locks down and you are busted.

Move with WASD / arrow keys or click to move. Synthesized WebAudio bleeps
only — no downloaded assets, no network requests. Pure static files under
`game/`; open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser and play.
